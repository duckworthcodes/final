The Char Dham

India's four sacred pilgrimage sites that every Hindu aspires to visit in a lifetime. Book pujas at all four.

🏔️
Badrinath
📍 Uttarakhand
The abode of Lord Vishnu in the Himalayas. One of the Char Dhams, open only in summer.
🌊
Dwarka
📍 Gujarat
The city built by Lord Krishna — a coastal temple of immense spiritual significance.
🏛️
Puri
📍 Odisha
Home of Lord Jagannath and the famous annual Rath Yatra — one of the world's largest religious gatherings.
🌅
Rameshwaram
